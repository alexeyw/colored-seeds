Privacy Policy

Colored Seeds for Android, by Aleksei Volodin. Effective 27 October 2026.

In short: Colored Seeds does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The game has no ads, no analytics and no account of its own, and it has no permission to access the internet.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your settings: sound, vibration, background, dealing mode, difficulty, color symbols.
  • The game in progress, so that you can resume it.
  • Game statistics used for achievements and leaderboards: pairs matched, rows cleared, games won and the best score for each difficulty.

This data stays in the game's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google Account, and restore it on a new device. You control this in Android settings.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services, provided by Google through Google Play services on your device, to:

  • sign you in with your Play Games profile;
  • unlock achievements and record your progress toward them;
  • submit your best score for each difficulty to the leaderboards.

Google processes this information and links it to your Play Games profile under the Google Privacy Policy. Leaderboards show your Play Games profile name to other players according to your Play Games settings. As the developer, we can see achievement and leaderboard statistics and leaderboard entries in Google Play Console; we do not receive your email address or other details of your Google Account. You can play without signing in.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may give developers aggregated statistics, such as installs, and — if you agreed to share usage and diagnostics data — anonymous crash and performance reports. These are covered by Google's policies.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the game stores on your device. Play Games data (achievements, scores, profile) can be managed or deleted in the Google Play Games settings or in your Google Account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
