Colored Seeds

A calm logic puzzle for Android: match pairs of colored seeds, clear rows and empty the board.

Get it on Google Play

How to play

  • Tap two seeds of the same color to remove them.
  • A pair can be matched when nothing stands between them: along a row, along a column, or from the end of one row to the start of the next.
  • A row that becomes empty disappears and earns bonus points.
  • Out of moves? Tap "+" to deal more seeds — every extra deal costs points.
  • Clear the whole board to win. The faster you finish, the bigger the time bonus.

Features

  • Three difficulty levels: 4, 6 or 9 colors.
  • Color symbols: an optional shape on every seed for players who find some colors hard to tell apart.
  • Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards.
  • Plays offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking.
